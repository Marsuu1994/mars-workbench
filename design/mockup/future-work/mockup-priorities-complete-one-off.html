Design · Priorities · decided 2026-09-23 · Option C

Finish a one-off without leaving the matrix

Today a one-off task can only be completed on the board, and only while a plan is active: track it, open the board, drag it to Done. With no active plan the matrix is a dead end. Decided: the existing “→” popover and the tap sheet become a Move to chooser with a third row, Done. Picking it completes the task on the spot — credited to the current week when a plan is active, a plain status change otherwise — and the card leaves the matrix. No confirm, no undo toast. Decision record, rejected options and the backend contract: design/spike/priorities-complete-one-off.md. This page is the UI spec until the priorities scenario page takes over.

Today — the dead end

The only exit from the matrix is “Track this week”, which needs a current-week active plan. Between plans (and for anyone who never made one) the send buttons disable, the hint bar warns, and the mobile sheet opens just to show two disabled targets. Even with a plan, closing a 1-point errand is a two-page trip. Flip the toggle to see both states.

Move-to chooser — Done joins Todo and In Progress

The chooser’s section label becomes Move to and it gains a Done row (success dot, check glyph) under a hairline. Without a plan the “→” stays enabled: the two track rows disable under the usual “No active plan yet” note and Done stays live — so the chooser finally has a purpose in the no-plan state. Tracked (“This Week”) cards show the “→” too, with only the Done row, since they are already on the board. Picking Done closes the chooser, removes the card and updates the counts. No toast, no undo.

Try it: click any “→” on desktop or tap any card on the phone, then pick a row. The plan toggle resets the frames.
